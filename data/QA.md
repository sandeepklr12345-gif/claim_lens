# Data QA (synthetic data)

All of `data/pairs.jsonl` and `data/cases/*.json` is **synthetic** (`source: "synthetic"`), generated by
`python -m claimlens.data.generate` with Ollama `qwen2.5:7b`, seed 42. No real personal data.

How labels were made (by construction, not by a human):
- **SUPPORTED case:** letter written from a restrictive clause (exclusion, waiting period, limit). Gold clause = that clause.
- **NOT_SUPPORTED case:** letter that wrongly rejects a treatment covered by a benefit clause. Gold clause = that benefit clause.
- **Pairs:** positive = (reason written from clause, that clause); hard negative = BM25-similar clause from a different section. A hard negative may occasionally also support the reason (label noise).
